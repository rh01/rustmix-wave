These Piper voices are refused because their dataset licenses do not permit
this use: en_US-lessac (Blizzard 2013 research-only), en_US-amy (Lessac
finetune), ja_JP-hi_fi_captain-medium (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), and
zh_CN-huayan-medium (dataset license unknown, Lessac finetune).

Generated clips are not committed. AUDIO.TXT on the card repeats the voice,
license, and attribution for the pack that was built.
